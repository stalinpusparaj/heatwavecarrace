## App content forms

**Ads:** Yes, the app contains ads (opt-in rewarded ads via Google AdMob).

**Target audience:** 13–15, 16–17 and 18+. Do not select any age under 13. "Appeals to children": No.

**Content rating (IARC questionnaire):**
- Category: Game.
- Violence: cartoon/arcade vehicle crashes only. Cars spin out and flash; no people are shown being hurt, no blood. Answer *No* to violence against humans and blood/gore.
- Fear, sexuality, language, drugs, gambling: No. There are no loot boxes or random paid items.
- Users can interact or exchange content: Yes. Crews (optional, signed in) share a crew name and co-op results with crew members. There is no free-text chat.
- Shares user location: No.
- Digital purchases: Yes.

Expected result: Teen / PEGI 7–12 range, depending on region.

**Data safety:**
