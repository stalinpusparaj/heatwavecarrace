# Visible campaign rescue stories

Every solo campaign level now has a deterministic identity (world + chapter location + character beat), pre-mission dispatch, in-play narrative ribbon, playable pickup and a homecoming message. The system combines five world arcs, ten district locations per world, ten recurring people per world and ten authored emotional beats. It yields 500 distinct titles/dispatch identities. It is a compositional story system, not 500 individually scripted cutscenes or unique mechanics.

Five rescue patterns reuse existing physics: rendezvous, a longer stationary pickup, two people at separate locations, supply recovery for a named person, and a smaller quiet-approach zone. After completing the existing objective, the player follows amber rescue markers, stays nearby to board passengers, and returns to a green home marker. Completion and normal campaign rewards require reaching home. Named people and world hubs appear in the result. Pickup cover reduces damage in a 100-unit zone to 45%; new patrol spawns pause during pickup, while existing police remain. This assistance is explained before accepting a mission.

All levels retain their prior unlock state and car upgrades. Old completed levels remain completed; replay can experience the new rescue phase. No new premium entitlement or reward currency is introduced. Existing once-only settlement and smaller replay credit policy remain. Intro stories, Rival Routes and live Convoy retain their separate rules. Rescue metadata is not processed per animation frame; story UI follows the graphics profile HUD cadence. Artwork is simple shapes; no new images, video, sound downloads or analytics SDKs.

Tests: `tests/rescue.cjs` checks all 500 identities/titles, all five patterns, objective→pickup→home gating and mode isolation. `tests/rescue-integration.cjs` uses the actual game and campaign to verify the objective alone cannot settle a win and homecoming grants credits once. `tests/campaign-playthrough.cjs docs/rescue-campaign-audit.json` simulates all 500 new mission flows with scripted steering, no teleporting and the starter car. Bot results are not human win rates; the extra travel/pickup phase increases difficulty and needs new-player testing. The former emulator baseline predates this story layer and must not be presented as testing this updated build.
